What this system does

It retrieves passages from the configured biomedical corpus, writes an answer as separate claims with citations, verifies every claim against the cited passage (support, contradiction, entities, numbers, citation resolution), checks that the answer is about the entities you asked about, and then decides whether to deliver it: ANSWER, ANSWER WITH CAUTION, CLARIFY, ABSTAIN or ESCALATE. It is not medical advice and does not answer patient-specific or dosing questions.

Known limits

  • Confidence is shown as uncalibrated until the calibration job has run for the served verifier.
  • Confusable-entity detection covers numbered families, prefixes, drug classes and a curated look-alike list; unrelated names not in that list are not caught.
  • Evidence quality is shown by tier (peer-reviewed, preprint, course material); a preprint is not peer-reviewed.
  • Human expert labels are collected from in-product feedback; until enough exist, reported metrics use proxy labels.
